Designing interfaces the way games are designed.

I'm Alwin, a UI/UX designer from Trivandrum, Kerala. I'm pursuing an M.Des in UI/UX at Lovely Professional University (2025–27), after a B.Des in Game Design. Along the way I designed game UI and 2D art as an intern at Sunday Games.

Games taught me that good interfaces give people a clear goal, instant feedback and a reason to keep going. I bring that to products, from mobile apps to everyday appliances: research first, then systems, then the small details that make something feel good to use.
